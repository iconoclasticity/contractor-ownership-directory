# Contractor Ownership Directory

A searchable directory of plumbing and HVAC contractors showing who owns each firm, for two regions:

- King County, Washington
- Colorado's Front Range, from Colorado Springs to Fort Collins

Many local plumbing and heating brands have been bought by private equity firms and kept their old names. This directory records ownership for each firm, with a source link for every private equity, franchise, corporate or independent label.

**This is a working draft.** Most smaller firms have not been checked, the Front Range research is at an early stage, and ownership changes often. "No evidence found" and "not yet checked" are not claims that a firm is independent. Open the cited sources before relying on any label.

## Where the data comes from

- **King County:** Washington State Department of Labor & Industries contractor licence data.
- **Front Range:** Colorado State Plumbing Board contractor licences and Colorado Secretary of State business records. Colorado does not license HVAC contractors at state level, so HVAC firms are found by business name.
- **Ownership:** acquisition press releases, owner and investor websites, trade press and state filings, linked from each entry.

## Corrections

If an entry is wrong or out of date, open an issue with a link to a source.
